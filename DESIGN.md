## Brand & Style

This design system translates the warmth of an artisan game lounge, local coffee shop, and personal collection vault into an inviting digital experience. The emotional tone is unhurried, reassuring, and deeply tactile—replacing clinical, high-tech tournament interfaces with the comforting atmosphere of polished oak tables, warm lighting, and treasured card sleeves.

The aesthetic blends **Modern Tactile Minimalist** with **Warm Craft Editorial**:
- **Tactile Comfort**: Surfaces evoke thick archival parchment, warm woods, and gentle ambient lamplight.
- **Approachable Craft**: Clean, contemporary typography balances rounded shapes with disciplined layout rhythm, making complex inventory and deck-building mechanics feel intuitive and serene.
- **Atmospheric Warmth**: High-contrast harsh blacks and stark sterile whites are banished in favor of deep roasted coffee undertones, rich honey amber, and soft clotted-cream canvas tones.

## Colors

The color palette draws directly from the warm logo canvas, dimensional gold timber typography, and rich roasted borders:

- **Primary (`#F29F38`)**: Warm honey amber. Used for hero calls to action, active indicators, rare inventory badges, and energetic accent points.
- **Secondary (`#D97706`)**: Toasted caramel. Provides grounded contrast for hover states, focused borders, subtle active accents, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary (`#382414`)**: Deep roasted espresso and dark chestnut. Serves as the high-contrast baseline for typography, iconography, and structured frames, replacing synthetic black with organic depth.
- **Neutral (`#FBF5EB`)**: Warm parchment cream. Acts as the primary page backdrop, with tinted surface layers stepping progressively down to `#F3E9D7` (card containers) and rising up to `#FFFDF9` (elevated modal sheets).

Support tints and status tones:
- **Success / Botanical Green**: `#4A7C59` (soft moss green for legal formats, deck passes, stock in-hand).
- **Alert / Mana Red**: `#C84C32` (terracotta ember for errors and destructive actions).
- **Muted Parchment Border**: `rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.08)` to `rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.14)`.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all roles to achieve an approachable, geometric warmth. Its rounded terminals and open apertures mirror the friendly curvature of table gaming tokens and archival card corners.

- **Display & Headlines**: Generous weights (700 and 800) with slight negative tracking echo the stout, reassuring presence of vintage woodblock mastheads without sacrificing digital crispness.
- **Body & Data**: Regular weights (400) set at generous line-heights (1.5x–1.6x) ensure long sessions of reading decklists, rulebooks, and pricing curves remain fatigue-free.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Formatted in medium and semi-bold weights with subtle tracking to maintain legibility when presenting mana costs, rarity indicators, and card quantities.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored on an 8pt spatial grid with generous breathing room, avoiding dense spreadsheet claustrophobia:

- **Desktop (>= 1024px)**: 12-column responsive grid with `gutter-lg` (32px) and `margin-lg` (48px). Max content container constrained to `1280px` for optimal reading comfort.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column grid with `gutter` (20px) and `margin` (24px). Dual-pane card browser collapses sidebars into slide-over canvas sheets.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with 16px to 20px outer margins. Complex deck tables transform into vertically stacked card vouchers.
- **Vertical Rhythm**: Section groupings use `space-xl` (36px) or larger; related component clusters use `space-md` (16px) to maintain a relaxed, organized pace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy relies on **tonal paper layering** complemented by **warm, amber-tinted ambient diffusion**. Harsh drop shadows are strictly avoided.

1. **Canvas Layer (`#FBF5EB`)**: The foundational parchment base.
2. **Resting Layer (Surface 1 - `#FFFDF9`)**: Raised card surfaces, drawers, and search bars. Outlined with a delicate warm chestnut stroke (`rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.08)`) and paired with an ambient shadow: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.05)`.
3. **Interactive & Hover Layer (Surface 2)**: Elevated collectibles and card previews lift gently: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.09)`.
4. **Overlay / Floating Sheets (Surface 3)**: Modals, filter sheets, and dropdown menus: `0 16px 40px -8px rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.14)`, enclosed in a 1px border of `#EAD9C4`.
5. **Inner Recessed Wells**: Search inputs and token slots utilize an inset tone (`#F3ECE0`) with a subtle top hairline border (`rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.06)`), creating the physical illusion of sunken card binders.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Rounded (`2`)** profile to deliver organic softness while maintaining clean architectural alignment:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px)**: Standard buttons, text inputs, filter tags, and thumbnail frames.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` - `1rem` / 16px)**: Content panels, collectible showcase cards, deck matrices, and dialogs.
- **Feature Radius (`rounded-xl` - `1.5rem` / 24px)**: Floating hero panels, trade lounge cards, and prominent promo units.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`)**: Count chips, rarity capsules, and status tags.
- **Dividers**: Gentle tapered hairline rules with centered decorative diamond pips or soft fading endpoints, mirroring vintage playing-card packaging.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid Honey Amber (`#F29F38`) fill, Deep Chestnut (`#382414`) text, weight 600, `rounded-md` (8px). Hover transitions to Toasted Caramel (`#D97706`) with a subtle warm glow (`0 4px 14px rgba(242, 159, 56, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary**: Tinted parchment fill (`#F3E9D7`) with a 1px border in `rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.12)`. Deep Chestnut text. Hover darkens to `#E9DCBF`.
- **Ghost**: No fill; Chestnut text with hover fill of `rgba(242, 159, 56, 0.12)`.

### Chips & Rarity Badges
- Compact capsules (`rounded-full`) with 12px height padding (`py-1 px-3`).
- **Mythic/Rare**: Honey amber tint background (`#FEF3E2`) with caramel text (`#B45309`) and warm border.
- **Common/Uncommon**: Soft neutral sand background (`#EFE5D5`) with deep chestnut text.
- Selected state turns solid `#382414` with `#FFFDF9` text.

### Cards & Collectible Vouchers
- Clean background `#FFFDF9` wrapped in a 1px border of `rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.08)`.
- Features an aspect-ratio-locked artwork viewport with smooth 8px corners.
- Lower metadata region includes Mana curve symbols, set icons, and crisp tabular numbers.
- Hover state: 4px lift, border shifts to `#F29F38`, shadow expands to warm amber ambiance.

### Inputs & Search
- Grounded in recessed ivory `#F3ECE0` with border `rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.12)`.
- Text color is `#382414` with placeholder tinted at 50% opacity.
- Focus state shifts background to `#FFFDF9` with a 2px outline in `#F29F38` and zero harsh blue browser halo.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- 20px boxes with 6px rounded corners (checkbox) or circular profiles (radio).
- Unchecked: `#EFE7D8` background with `rgba(56, 36, 20, 0.25)` outline.
- Checked: `#F29F38` fill displaying an espresso `#382414` check icon or dot.

### Specialized Vault Elements
- **Deck Stats Ribbon**: Segmented bars showing mana distribution in desaturated earth tones (Plains: `#FAF4E6`, Island: `#4A6D88`, Swamp: `#2C2623`, Mountain: `#C45A45`, Forest: `#4E7A5A`).
- **Binder Strip Dividers**: Thin horizontal separators featuring an ornamental centered notch or diamond emblem, honoring traditional craft stationery.
